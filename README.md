## Why some labs are in separate repositories

Lab 4, the CI/CD calculator lab, Lab 5 and Lab 6 were built on starter or
template repositories, so each lives in its own repository. Lab 4 and the
calculator lab are linked only. For Lab 5 and Lab 6 the main files are
copied here as well, next to the link.
